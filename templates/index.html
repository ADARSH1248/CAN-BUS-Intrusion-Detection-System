About the System

Understanding CAN Bus Security

A deep dive into vehicle communication and why protecting it matters.

What is CAN Bus?

The Controller Area Network (CAN) Bus is a robust vehicle communication protocol that allows microcontrollers and devices to communicate without a host computer. It connects ECUs like the engine, brakes, steering, and infotainment into a single network.

Why is Security Critical?

Modern vehicles are increasingly connected to the internet and smartphones. The CAN Bus was designed without security in mind, making it vulnerable to injection, spoofing, and DoS attacks. A compromised CAN Bus lets an attacker control braking, steering, or engine functions remotely.
